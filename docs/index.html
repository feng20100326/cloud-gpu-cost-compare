TL;DR

  • Validate an idea → Colab / Kaggle free tier ($0, but see the official quotes).
  • Job must finish → per-second rental: a 4090-class card is around $1/hour, so an interruption costs cents.
  • Lowest price, resumable work → SaladCloud "Lowest" tier: $0.015/hr — and the most interruptible.
  • 48–80 GB VRAM → compare at that tier; that is where the real price spread lives.
  • Price shopping / on-off flexibility → hourly marketplaces (e.g. Clore.ai) — machines are third-party, so no vendor SLA.

Read this before quoting $0.015.
SaladCloud's $0.015/hr is the official "Lowest" priority starting price (RTX 3060 / 3070 / 3070 Ti class). The same official page states, in its own words:
  • "Lowest is the lowest cost and the most interruptible"
  • "May be preempted by higher-priority groups. Minimum run before reallocation is roughly 30 minutes."
  • Billed per second.
Comparing $0.015/hr against a $0.69+/hr hosted card compares the cheapest-and-most-likely-to-be-killed tier with a stable hosted machine. Running a long 4090-class job on that tier, being preempted is expected. It suits batch work you can resume — not a job that must finish in one go.
